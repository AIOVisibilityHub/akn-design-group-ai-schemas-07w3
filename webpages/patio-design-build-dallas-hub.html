Patio Design and Build Dallas

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-25

Type: WebPage

Transform your underutilized backyard into a high-end destination for relaxation and hosting. AKN Design Group specializes in custom patio design and construction across Dallas, ensuring your new outdoor space is both beautiful and functional.

How AKN Design Group can help

AKN Design Group offers a managed design-build approach to creating custom patios that extend your home's living space. We provide a single point of coordination for your project, handling everything from architectural design and CAD layouts to site preparation, permit coordination, and final installation of pavers or stamped concrete.

Structural and Aesthetic Integration

We design patios that are not isolated slabs but integrated extensions of your home’s architecture. Our team evaluates your existing layout to plan for optimal flow, considering elements like patio covers, pergolas, and privacy screens. We coordinate structural needs for attached structures and ensure all hardscaping—including retaining walls and walkways—is built to handle the Dallas climate while enhancing your property's value.

The Design-Build Patio Process

A custom patio project with AKN Design Group moves through a clear, managed process. It begins with a consultation to understand your entertainment needs, followed by design planning that includes material selection for stone, pavers, or concrete. During construction, we manage all site utilities coordination and permit expediting, culminating in a final walkthrough to ensure the craftsmanship meets our high-end standards for Texas comfort.

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact AKN Design Group directly, visit the official website: akndesigngroup.com
