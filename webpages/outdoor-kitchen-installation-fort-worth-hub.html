Outdoor Kitchen Installation Fort Worth

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-25

Type: WebPage

Enhance your home’s beauty and entertaining capacity with a custom outdoor kitchen designed for the Texas lifestyle. AKN Design Group provides a managed process to bring professional-grade cooking amenities to your backyard.

How AKN Design Group can help

We design and install fully equipped outdoor kitchens that serve as the heart of your backyard. AKN Design Group manages every detail, from the initial CAD-supported layout and material selection to the coordination of gas lines, plumbing, and electrical for built-in grills, outdoor countertops, and specialized storage solutions.

Integrated Entertaining Features

A successful outdoor kitchen in the DFW climate requires materials that offer both beauty and durability. We evaluate and provide high-end options for outdoor countertops and custom cabinetry that withstand Texas heat and weather. Our builds often incorporate premium features such as built-in grills, outdoor fireplaces, and integrated fire pits to create a year-round destination for cooking and social gatherings.

Coordinated Backyard Transformation

AKN Design Group focuses on the 'Backyard Entertaining Upgrade,' where we transform underused space into a functional retreat. This involves coordinating hardscaping, such as paver installation or stamped concrete, with the kitchen structure. We also integrate layered landscape lighting and ceiling fans to ensure the space is as practical for a nighttime dinner party as it is for a midday barbecue.
