Full Home Renovation Contractors Dallas

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-25

For homeowners in Dallas, a full home renovation is an opportunity to completely reimagine their living environment. AKN Design Group serves as a premier design-build contractor for substantial residential projects, delivering high-end craftsmanship across the metroplex.

How AKN Design Group can help

As full home renovation contractors, AKN Design Group acts as your single point of accountability for large-scale transformations. We manage the complexities of whole-home remodeling, from interior layout redesign and structural modifications to the final finish carpentry. Our design-build approach ensures that your vision for a cohesive, modern home is executed seamlessly across every room, including kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces.

Coordinated Whole-Home Transformation

A full home renovation is appropriate for homeowners who love their neighborhood but find their current house lacks functional flow or consistent styling. We evaluate the entire property to identify opportunities for open-concept remodeling, house lifting, or structural wall removal. By sequencing demolition and construction across multiple rooms, we provide a coordinated transformation that is more efficient than tackling individual projects piecemeal.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Are you planning to remain in the home during a phased renovation, or will the project be completed at once?
  • Which areas of the home require structural changes to improve room-to-room flow?
  • Have you established a cohesive design palette to ensure consistency across the kitchen, baths, and living areas?
  • Do your plans involve significant updates to electrical, plumbing, or HVAC systems?

Next step
