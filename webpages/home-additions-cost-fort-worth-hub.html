How AKN Design Group can help

AKN Design Group helps Fort Worth homeowners navigate the complexities of home extensions, from initial cost estimating to final construction. Our design-build approach ensures that your primary suite, sunroom, or second-story addition is planned with value engineering in mind, keeping the project aligned with your budget while maintaining high-end craftsmanship.

Structural Integration and Cost Factors

The cost of a home addition in Fort Worth is determined by the scope of structural integration. Second-story additions and primary suite extensions require meticulous foundation construction and roofline integration to ensure the new space feels like a natural part of the original home. AKN Design Group provides a managed process that includes CAD-supported layouts to visualize the addition before construction begins, allowing for accurate budget planning.

The AKN Design-Build Process for Additions

A successful addition requires professional coordination of foundation construction, framing, and HVAC extensions. AKN Design Group handles all site utilities coordination and permit expediting in the DFW metroplex. We focus on 'More Space Without Moving,' ensuring the exterior finish matches and the interior flow is optimized for your family’s needs.
